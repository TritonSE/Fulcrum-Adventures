Privacy & support

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy covers the Fulcrum Adventures mobile app. You can browse activities without creating an account. Here is how the app handles your information.

Your saved activities and notes

Bookmarks, playlists, viewing history, downloaded activity content, notes, ratings, and dates are saved on your device. The app does not upload this personal library to Fulcrum or sync it between devices. You can remove saved items or edit notes in the app. Device backups may include this data; clearing app data or deleting the app removes its local copy.

Optional mailing list

If you sign up, your email address and signup record are stored in Fulcrum’s mailing list so staff can send activities, guides, and updates. Signing up is optional. Your address remains on the list until it is removed. To withdraw consent or request removal, contact us using the details below.

Online services and videos

The app loads content through hosting and storage providers, including Vercel, MongoDB, and Google Firebase. These services may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, and device or browser details to deliver and protect the service. Technical logs follow the providers’ configured retention settings.

Video thumbnails and embedded videos connect to YouTube. Google may process connection information, identifiers, and cookies under its Privacy Policy.

Questions or need a hand?

Contact Fulcrum for app help, privacy questions, or mailing-list removal. For a bug, include your device, app version, and what happened. For email removal, include the address you used to sign up.
